## Specific claims to challenge experimentally

“Stateless means no server state”: verify a JWT, then revoke a membership in stage 17. The signature is still valid; the current decision changes. “Backend apps do not need PKCE”: confidential client authentication and code-transaction proof protect different bindings; follow the published security BCP. “SCIM removes access everywhere in minutes”: stop delivery or leave an offline JWT verifier running; find the missing revocation mechanism and bound. “SSO grants access to all apps”: link the authenticated subject without a membership; default deny must remain.
